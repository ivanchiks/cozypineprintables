How to make a paper dollhouse room

Print the template at 100% ("Actual size"). Cut out the back wall and floor and the two side walls, fold the back wall up along the dashed line, and glue the side walls onto the grey tabs. Now you have an open room box, 5.4 x 2.9 x 3.3 in (13.7 x 7.4 x 8.4 cm).

Next come the furniture sheets. Each piece has a number, and a small diagram shows where it goes in the room. Fold every piece into a little box, glue it, and set it in place. Cardstock (160-200 gsm, roughly 65-110 lb) keeps the furniture from squashing.

Printing and gluing tips

  • Choose the US Letter or A4 file that matches your paper and print single-sided at 100%.
  • Use cardstock for the room box; thinner paper is fine for the small furniture.
  • Score the fold lines against a ruler and a blunt pen so the walls fold cleanly.
  • Let glue dry for a minute before adding the next piece.

Frequently asked questions

Is this paper dollhouse template really free?

Yes. The Christmas Morning Bedroom is a free 6-page PDF with the room template, furniture and steps. The full dollhouse with four rooms is a paid set.

What paper should I use?

Cardstock, 65-110 lb (160-200 gsm), is best for the room box. Printer paper works for a trial run.

Do kids need to read the instructions?

Not much. The steps are pictures with a short phrase each, and the furniture is numbered, so kids can follow the pictures from step 1 to step 6.
